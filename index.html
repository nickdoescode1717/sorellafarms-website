The Estate Legacy

Where History Meets Eternal Celebration

Nestled among thirty acres of rolling farmland in Niagara's Wine Country, Sorella 1881 has been a place of gathering for six generations. What began as a working farm in 1881 has become one of the region's most cherished wedding estates.

History breathes through every beam and bloom here. We are not a replica of something rustic — we are the real thing, lovingly preserved and thoughtfully reimagined for the couples who choose to begin their lives at Sorella.
