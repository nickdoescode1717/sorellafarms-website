Weddings

A day written entirely in your own words

Your Vision, Our Devotion

A Wedding as Unique as Your Love

At Sorella 1881, we believe a wedding should feel like the truest expression of who you are together. Not a template — a living, breathing celebration shaped by your story, your people, and the details that matter most to you.

From the moment you first walk the estate to the last dance of the evening, our team is here to ensure every element unfolds with ease, warmth, and elegance. We hold just a limited number of weddings each season so that every couple receives the full devotion of the Sorella team.

The Ceremony

Where Your Vows Come to Life

Choose from multiple ceremony settings across the estate. Exchange your vows against the ivy-covered wall of the 1881 barn, beneath a canopy of ancient trees, or in the serene garden meadow as Niagara's open skies stretch above you.

  • The Barn Courtyard — an intimate ceremony backdrop framed by climbing ivy and lush greenery
  • Garden Ceremony — open-air vows beneath mature trees with rolling farmland beyond
  • The Pond — a serene waterside setting bathed in natural light
The Reception

Celebrate Under the Open Sky

Sorella 1881's reception spaces are as versatile as they are beautiful. Host an al fresco feast in the twinkle-lit Courtyard, or gather under the soaring white canopy of the Blanc Marquee for an evening of dancing and dining.

Blanc Marquee

Elegant white marquee with wraparound views — up to 240 guests

The Courtyard

Al fresco dining by the barn under café lights — up to 170 guests

The Pond

A panoramic lakeside setting for outdoor celebrations — up to 240 guests

Farm to Table

The Details That Linger

Great weddings are made in the details — the candlelight on the table, the scent of seasonal blooms, the first bite of something extraordinary. At Sorella, every element of your table is considered with the same care we give to the estate itself.

Through our culinary partner La Dolce Sera, we bring the harvest of Niagara directly to your table. Farm-to-table menus are crafted seasonally, celebrating the flavours of the region that has shaped Sorella for over a century.

  • Farm-to-table dining
  • Seasonal menus
  • Local Niagara wines
  • Custom florals
  • Curated rentals
  • Lighting & décor
The Evening

Dance Until the Stars Come Out

When dinner gives way to dancing, the Blanc Marquee transforms. Whether it's a live band beneath our soaring white canopy or an intimate string quartet in the courtyard, the evening at Sorella is one your guests will speak about for years.

On-Site Accommodations

Stay the Night at The Ranch House

For the wedding party who wants to savour every moment, The Ranch House sleeps up to twelve guests on-site. With a barrel sauna, hot tub, fire pits, and a beautifully appointed interior, it is the perfect retreat before — and after — the celebration.
