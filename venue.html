Spaces That Tell Your Story

Every corner of Sorella 1881 has been shaped by time, family, and intention. From the ivy-draped 1881 barn to the sprawling Blanc Marquee, each space offers a distinct mood — and every one is available exclusively to you on your wedding day.

The Courtyard

Nestled between the historic barn and the estate gardens, the Courtyard is where Sorella's magic is most felt. String lights trace the roofline above you, the scent of evening blooms drifts through the air, and the weathered barn wall becomes your backdrop.

Ideal for al fresco dinners, cocktail hours, and ceremonies for up to 170 guests. The Courtyard flows seamlessly to the Blanc Marquee for larger celebrations.

Up to 240 Guests

Blanc Marquee

Soaring white canvas walls, soft natural light filtering through arched windows, and an open-sided terrace that brings the vineyard landscape inside — the Blanc Marquee is Sorella's grandest event space.

Fully weather-protected and elegantly finished, the Marquee seats up to 240 guests and offers a dance floor, full catering access, and a dedicated cocktail terrace at sunset.

Intimate Gatherings

The 1881 Barn

The heart of the estate. Built in 1881 and still dressed each summer in cascading ivy, the original barn is more than a backdrop — it is the soul of Sorella. Weathered wood, steeply pitched rooflines, and a timeless presence that no new construction could replicate.

The barn's north-facing wall serves as the most sought-after ceremony altar on the estate — a living wall of green against the open sky, framed by seasonal florals.
