Our Approach

No Two Weddings Are the Same

At Sorella 1881, we don't believe in rigid packages — we believe in building each wedding from the ground up around the couple in front of us. The size of your celebration, the spaces you choose, the services you need: all of these shape a custom investment that reflects your day, not a template.

We hold a limited number of weddings each season, which means when you choose Sorella, you receive the full attention of our entire team. Reach out to receive our detailed wedding package and begin a conversation about your vision.

On-Site Wedding Planning

Planning by the People Who Know This Place Best

Carly and Hayley — the sisters behind Sorella 1881 — are also the founders of Halo House, a boutique wedding planning and design studio. No one knows this estate more intimately. No one is better positioned to bring your vision to life within it.

As certified Wedding Planners Institute of Canada members, they handle every layer of the process: vendor sourcing, budget and timeline management, design direction, rehearsal coordination, and full day-of presence. Their philosophy is simple — the event should feel as good as it looks.
